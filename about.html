A renovation company built around practical construction experience, disciplined planning and professional project delivery.

Our Approach

Good renovation work starts before the tools come out.

MYEC Inc. brings structure to the renovation process.

Our leadership background spans civil engineering, construction, site coordination and project management. That experience informs how we review existing conditions, define scope, coordinate details and communicate expectations.

The goal is straightforward: understand the work, plan it clearly, execute it carefully and finish it properly.

What guides our work

  • Clear written scope and assumptions
  • Practical planning before construction
  • Direct communication with the client
  • Respect for occupied homes and properties
  • Attention to fit, finish and transitions
  • Professional Engineer-led leadership

How We Work

Professionalism throughout the project, not only at the end.

Scope Discipline

We work to clarify what is included, what is excluded and which assumptions affect the price or sequence.

Construction Awareness

Existing conditions, access, materials and sequencing are considered before the work moves forward.

Finish Quality

Preparation, alignment, transitions, trim, paint and final presentation are treated as part of the scope.
